Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. You add the dates, notes and reminder times yourself. If you turn on phone notifications, your phone's notification address is stored so we can reach it.

How we use it

Only to run Wishly: to show your dates, send reminders by email or phone notification, and let you share dates and chat with circles you join. We do not sell your data, show ads or share your details with anyone else.

Circle chat

Circle messages are encrypted in your browser. Only people who have the circle's invite link can read them.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored with our hosting provider (Netlify). Emails are sent through EmailJS. Sign-in is provided by Google Firebase.

Your choices

You can change your email or turn off notifications in Settings at any time. To delete your account and data, email the developer at the address below and it will be removed.
